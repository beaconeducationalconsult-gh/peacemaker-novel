# Mallam Town — Book Overview

## Title

✅ **Decided: *The Peacemaker*.** "Mallam Town" continues as the setting name (used throughout the Story Bible file names and as shorthand for the community itself) but is no longer the book title. Foregrounds Kwame and the central irony — his defining trait becomes the evidence against him.

## Subgenre

Drama, Thriller — specifically a **wrongful-accusation crime thriller with a social-realist community backdrop**. Closer in spirit to small-town-secrets crime fiction than to procedural/detective fiction — the investigation matters, but the emotional engine is a good man's reputation being destroyed by forces (gossip, land-guard economics, his own silence) larger than any single villain.

## Logline

*When a peaceful taxi station master is wrongly accused of nearly killing his wife and linked to a murder he had nothing to do with, the same instinct that made him beloved — his refusal to explain or defend himself — becomes the evidence that could cost him his freedom.*

(Working version — refine once Saaba's characterization is locked, since that will sharpen it further. Title is now settled, so the logline no longer needs to hedge on that.)

## Central mystery / spine question

**What really happened to Kwame, Mansa, and Saaba on the same night?** Per the Mystery/Clue Ledger, the reader should initially believe these are three separate incidents; the book's structure exists to slowly reveal they're one story.

## Tone & comparable works

## POV structure

✅ **Decided: single POV, Kwame only, throughout.** This is a real structural commitment — the Chapter Outline has been fully reworked accordingly (Ch. 3, 7, 9, 10, 11, 18–21, 22–25 all previously assumed access to other characters' heads and have been converted to secondhand/testimony/direct-conversation techniques instead). Two consequences worth knowing before drafting: Ch. 10 and 11 no longer stand alone as chapters (folded into Ch. 14), and Act IV runs as "accused man learns what's happening to him" rather than a procedural — see the Chapter Outline's top section for the full breakdown and the honest trade-offs involved.
