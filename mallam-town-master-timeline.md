### ✅ DECIDED — What happens between Kwame leaving and Kwame returning

**Locked in: option (b).** He sits at the junction/Last Stop (same location as the barrier he saw the Camry break through that afternoon), gets a call about stranded passengers at the station, and returns home to get his car. This costs some immediate suspense (no red herring of "he forgot his phone") but buys more meaning — it's his sense of duty as station master, the same trait that got him robbed of ₵300 that morning, that puts him back on the road alone at night. The tragic irony is worth the trade. (See the consolidated table below, under "Exact moment Mansa falls," for how this interleaves with her timeline.)

### ✅ DECIDED — Exact moment Mansa falls

**Locked in:** Mansa — **acting on Abena's earlier advice to follow him** — attempts to sneak out through the window immediately after Kwame leaves the room — so she falls *before* his search, not during or after it. Her phone goes dead as a direct result of the fall (this is why it's unreachable when Kwame calls it, not because she switched it off). Someone in the compound hears the impact — the bang of her hitting the ground — but doesn't know where it came from and ignores it. That's a clean, plantable clue: a witness who unknowingly heard the one sound that would have changed everything, and said nothing because it didn't seem worth mentioning at the time.

By the time Kwame returns and searches the room, she is already lying unconscious just outside the window — he doesn't check outside, and calls a phone that's already dead from the fall, not from her having switched it off.

### ✅ DECIDED — Mechanism of the car theft

**Locked in:** it's not a robbery of an unconscious man — it's a staged carjacking that escalates because Kwame, true to character, refuses even at gunpoint. Three robbers (two men, one woman) flag him down asking for a ride. He turns them down twice — first plainly, then even against an offer of extra money — because he's preoccupied with finding Mansa. The leader pulls a gun; Kwame *still* doesn't comply, which is what tips it into violence: the leader reaches into the car to kill the ignition, hauls the door open, drags him out, and the group beats him unconscious before dumping him and driving off in the car. This is a strong beat to dramatize directly on the page — it's actually another quiet demonstration of Kwame's character (immovable even under a gun, for his wife's sake) right before it gets him nearly killed, which lands hard against the egg-seller scene from earlier that same day.

## ✅ DECIDED — The Cemetery

**Locked in:** the cemetery is the outside land-guard crew's staging ground/hideout — not supernatural. The scene keeps its full dread (Nanfuri's exhaustion, the stillness that "didn't feel empty," his instinct to step back and say nothing) because that dread is earned and correct, not because anything otherworldly is present. What he actually senses on Day 0 — cleared paths, a stash point, signs of use — he doesn't yet have the context to name. He makes the professional call to withdraw rather than go in alone against armed men who just shot out his tyres, and says only "Carefully."

This closes the loop structurally: the eventual arrest of the robbers (once Chocho identifies them via the recording) happens **at or returning to this same cemetery**, this time with Nanfuri backed up rather than alone. What read as unexplained and eerie in Day 0's opening act becomes, by the end, the literal site of the resolution — and gives Nanfuri a private thread worth carrying the whole book: he saw the answer on night one and chose restraint over pursuit, a decision that deserves to be revisited once it's his own daughter's death he's investigating — now compounded by the fact that **he was on the phone with her when she was attacked**.

## Notes on tightness

This entire spine — attack, robbery, murder, discovery, manhunt, capture — happens inside roughly **40 hours** (Day 0 evening to Day 2 daytime). That's a genuine strength (relentless pace) but it means every scene needs its timestamp checked against this table before it's drafted, or you risk exactly the kind of slip flagged earlier (manhunt starting at 4:30 p.m. only works because Kwame wakes at 4:15 p.m. — a 15-minute margin that has to be protected, not accidentally widened or narrowed in later drafts).

**Geography tightened 2026-09-15:** Last Stop = junction/barrier (one location). Saaba's house = 7 houses from that junction (short drive for stolen car). Uncompleted building = bush zone near Nene's hut, distinct from cemetery east of Kwame's house. Keep these separate in prose to avoid conflation.
